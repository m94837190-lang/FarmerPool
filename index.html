About FarmerPool

Problem

Small farmers may have limited access to buyers, transparent price information, and cost-effective transport. Buyers may need to contact many farmers to source sufficient quantities.

Proposed solution

FarmerPool provides a shared crop-listing marketplace where farmers publish crop, quantity, expected price, and location, while buyers browse and compare listings. Future versions can support verified buyer requests, pooled orders, and transport coordination.

Prototype scope

  • Responsive mobile-friendly interface
  • Sample listings and crop/location search
  • Form to add a listing, saved locally in the browser
  • Basic validation and clear demo limitations

Planned production features

  • Farmer/buyer accounts and verified profiles
  • Live mandi-price data from approved sources with timestamp and source
  • Order acceptance, cancellation rules, and dispute handling
  • Transport partner availability, fallback plans, and transparent costs
  • Regional languages, low-bandwidth support, and optional SMS alerts
  • Secure backend, access control, audit logs, backups, and privacy controls
